# MESSAGE FROM THE CHAIRMAN

**Mopuri Aushish Kumar**
**Chairman, Sri Mathaa Yellamanba Service Trust**

It gives me immense pleasure and a deep sense of responsibility to serve as the Chairman of **Sri Mathaa Yellamanba Service Trust**.

My educational journey in **B.Tech – Electronics and Communication Engineering** from Annamacharya Institute of Technology and Sciences, Hyderabad, followed by an **MBA from Narayana College, Tadipatri**, has given me a combination of technical knowledge, management skills and an understanding of organizational development.

Over the years, my professional journey has provided me with valuable experience in **construction, manufacturing of cement products such as fencing poles and CC bricks, and the restaurant business**. These experiences have taught me the importance of hard work, planning, quality, employment generation and responsible management.

My involvement in public and professional activities, including my experience as **Professional Wing President in the Dharmavaram Assembly Constituency**, has further strengthened my understanding of the challenges faced by communities and the importance of working together for meaningful development.

At **Sri Mathaa Yellamanba Service Trust**, our objective is not merely to provide temporary assistance, but to create opportunities that can bring lasting positive change in people's lives. We believe that **education, healthcare, skill development, women empowerment, child welfare, community development and support for vulnerable sections of society** are essential pillars of a stronger and more inclusive society.

As Chairman, I am committed to building an organization that works with **transparency, accountability, compassion and dedication**. We aim to collaborate with communities, educational institutions, government bodies, corporate organizations, volunteers and like-minded individuals to take our service initiatives to people who need them most.

I strongly believe that social service is not the responsibility of one individual or one organization. It is a collective responsibility. When people, institutions and communities come together with a common purpose, even small efforts can create a significant impact.

Our vision is to expand the reach of Sri Mathaa Yellamanba Service Trust and develop sustainable programmes that empower individuals and communities to become self-reliant and confident.

**Together, let us work towards a society where every child has access to education, every individual has an opportunity to grow, and every community receives the support it deserves.**

With commitment, compassion and collective effort, **Sri Mathaa Yellamanba Service Trust** will continue to serve society and work towards a better and more equitable future.

**Mopuri Aushish Kumar**
**Chairman**
**Sri Mathaa Yellamanba Service Trust**
